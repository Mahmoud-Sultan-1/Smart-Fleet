The Problem

Venues Are Losing the Engagement Battle

Hospitality venues struggle with low dwell time, weak loyalty, and zero data on guest preferences. Customers scroll their phones instead of connecting with the brand.

🕑

Low Dwell Time

Average cafe visits last under 30 minutes. Short visits mean lower revenue per customer and underutilized seats.

👤

Weak Loyalty

Paper stamp cards and generic apps fail to keep customers engaged. Repeat visit rates remain stubbornly low.

📊

Zero Guest Data

Venues have no insight into preferences, visit patterns, or what drives repeat visits. They fly blind on every decision.
