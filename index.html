In Development

Noor — The Face of Hospitality

A humanoid reception robot with lifelike expressions, designed for hotel lobbies, corporate offices, and government service centers. Noor greets visitors by name, manages check-ins, provides wayfinding, and delivers concierge services — all in fluent Arabic dialect.

  • Hotels: Welcome guests, manage check-in queues, recommend services, and provide 24/7 lobby presence.
  • Corporate Offices: Visitor registration, badge printing, meeting room wayfinding, and employee notifications.
  • Government Centers: Queue management, form assistance, multilingual support for citizens and residents.
  • Healthcare: Patient check-in, department wayfinding, and appointment scheduling at hospital reception.
In Development

Saqr — Service & Delivery

An autonomous multi-compartment service robot built for indoor logistics. Saqr navigates complex environments to deliver room service, transport goods between floors, and provide on-demand assistance — tirelessly and precisely.

  • Hotels: Room service delivery, amenity transport, and housekeeping supply runs — 24/7 with no complaints.
  • Restaurants: Food delivery from kitchen to table, dirty dish return, and multi-table serving in busy hours.
  • Hospitals: Medicine delivery, lab sample transport, and supply distribution across departments.
  • Malls & Airports: Passenger assistance, last-mile delivery, and patrol — autonomous and always on.
