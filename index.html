Autonomous Inspection & Monitoring

MiniBot navigates complex environments autonomously, using its sensor array to inspect equipment, detect anomalies, and report findings in real-time. Ideal for warehouse inventory, facility monitoring, and perimeter security.

  • ✓ Thermal anomaly detection with 0.1°C accuracy
  • ✓ Automated inventory counting via computer vision
  • ✓ Gas leak and environmental hazard sensing
  • ✓ Scheduled patrol routes with deviation alerts

$ minibot inspect --zone warehouse-A

Smart Fleet Coordination

Multiple MiniBots work as a coordinated swarm, communicating through mesh networking to cover large areas efficiently. The Smart Fleet platform acts as the central nervous system for task orchestration.

  • ✓ Shared spatial mapping across all units
  • ✓ Automatic load balancing and battery management

Predictive Maintenance Intelligence

MiniBot continuously monitors its own systems and surrounding equipment using AI models trained on millions of operational data points. It predicts failures before they happen, reducing downtime by up to 40%.

  • ✓ Vibration analysis for motor and bearing health
  • ✓ Visual defect detection using deep learning
  • ✓ Predictive failure scoring with 90%+ accuracy
  • ✓ Automated maintenance scheduling integration

Development Roadmap

The Path Forward

From prototype to production — our roadmap to deploying Physical AI at scale.

Phase 1 — Foundation

Core Hardware & Perception

Custom PCB design, sensor integration, motor control systems, and the foundational perception stack. SLAM navigation in controlled indoor environments.

Phase 2 — Intelligence

AI Models & Autonomy

Training and deploying on-device ML models for object detection, anomaly recognition, and predictive maintenance. Autonomous task execution without human oversight.

Phase 3 — Integration

Fleet Platform & Multi-Robot Ops

Deep integration with Smart Fleet platform. Multi-robot coordination, mesh networking, and centralized fleet management dashboard.

Phase 4 — Scale

Pilot Deployments & Production

Pilot programs with strategic partners across logistics, oil & gas, and construction sectors. Manufacturing optimization and production-grade hardware.

Phase 5 — Expand

Regional Expansion & Ecosystem

Full-scale deployment across KSA and GCC. Developer SDK, third-party integrations, and an open ecosystem for industry-specific modules.
